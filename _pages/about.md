I am a Research Fellow at [City-REDI](https://www.birmingham.ac.uk/research/city-redi), University of Birmingham, where I have been since February 2022. I completed my PhD in Economics at the University of Birmingham in 2022, following an MSc in Economics from the University of Manchester and a BSc in Development Economics from SOAS, University of London.

My research sits at the intersection of **regional economics, innovation, firm behaviour and policy evaluation**. Recent and ongoing work covers the regional innovation paradox and absorptive capacity, the regional economic effects of the UK's R&D Levelling-Up mission, advanced digital skills and wage returns, green procurement and supply-chain innovation, and the political economy of pollution control in China. I have led an externally funded project on digital skills shortages in the UK and contribute as Co-I or researcher to projects funded by AHRC, Research England, and the Connected Places Catapult.

I aim to produce research that is both academically rigorous and useful to policy. My work has been presented at the Royal Economic Society Annual Conference, the Geography of Innovation Conference, the Regional Science Association, and other international venues, and has informed reports for the BBC, the West Midlands Combined Authority, and the Greater Birmingham Chambers of Commerce.
